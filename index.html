§ 01 / About

A bridge between quantum theory
and real-world machines.

I hold a PhD in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Bologna, completed in close collaboration with Ferrari S.p.A. on quantum computing applied to the kinematic and geometric optimization of double-wishbone suspension systems. Successfully defended in March 2026, my thesis explores how QUBO formulations, variational quantum circuits, and classical metaheuristics can co-exist in a single hybrid pipeline.

I spent eight months as a Visiting Research Scholar at Purdue University Fort Wayne and serve as an IBM Qiskit Advocate. Beyond suspension geometry, my work spans time-series forecasting for healthcare, computer vision, federated learning, and decision-support systems — with thirty-plus peer-reviewed publications across Springer, IEEE, MDPI, Wiley, and Elsevier venues.

§ 03 / Research Domains

Six threads that knot together.

My work is unified by one question: how do we make optimization tractable when the design space is geometric, continuous, and physically constrained?

01↗
ψ

Quantum Computing

QUBO formulations, QAOA, variational quantum algorithms, and D-Wave annealing for combinatorial design problems.

02↗
∇

Hybrid Optimization

QUBO–SQP and surrogate-assisted frameworks bridging discrete quantum sampling with continuous classical refinement.

03↗
⚙

Automotive Kinematics

3D parametric modeling and multi-objective optimization of double-wishbone and five-link suspensions.

04↗
∂

Deep Learning

Time-series forecasting, computer vision, and reinforcement learning for design and clinical decision support.

05↗
∑

Decision Sciences

MCDM techniques (MAIRCA, MOORA, MARCOS) for evaluation and ranking in operational and educational settings.

Quantum machine learning for cardiac phenotyping, cancer prediction, and biomedical signal classification.
