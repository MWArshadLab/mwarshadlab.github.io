About

Quantum & AI methods that ship to real systems

I recently completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Bologna, with a doctoral project carried out in industrial collaboration with Ferrari S.p.A. (Maranello). My thesis — Geometric and Kinematic Modeling of Double-Wishbone Suspensions with Advanced AI–Quantum Optimization Techniques — was successfully defended in March 2026.

My Ph.D. was supervised by Prof. Stefano Lodi at Bologna. During my doctorate I spent eight months as a Visiting Research Scholar at Purdue University (Fort Wayne, IN, USA) under Prof. David Q. Liu, working on quantum computing, machine learning, and computer-vision applications.

My research program spans QUBO formulations and quantum annealing, variational quantum algorithms, hybrid quantum–classical solvers, multi-objective and surrogate-assisted optimization, graph neural networks with calibrated LLM supervision, digital twins for vehicle dynamics, and AI for healthcare. I have authored 35+ peer-reviewed publications across IEEE, Springer, Elsevier, Wiley, and MDPI venues.

I am an IBM Qiskit Advocate, an IEEE Member, a Professional ACM Member, and General Secretary of the Internet Society Blockchain Special Interest Group.

Research

Research areas

Quantum & hybrid optimization

QUBO formulations, quantum annealing (D-Wave), variational quantum algorithms, and hybrid quantum–classical pipelines (QUBO–SQP, VQA-assisted multi-objective design) for hard engineering optimization.

AI for engineering & digital twins

Kinematic modeling and multi-objective optimization of vehicle suspensions, surrogate-assisted design, reinforcement learning for geometry search, and HPC benchmarking of metaheuristics.

Graphs, calibration & LLMs

Calibration-aware gating for budgeted LLM supervision in GNN link prediction; uncertainty quantification, selective prediction, and efficient human/LLM-in-the-loop learning.

Quantum machine learning

QML for biomedical signals and imaging — including classification of left-ventricular ejection-fraction phenotypes from echocardiograms — and quantum approaches in the health sector.

Healthcare AI

Drug-discovery optimization, cancer prediction from RNA sequencing, medical imaging segmentation (YOLOv11-SAMNet), and clinical decision support with symbolic-numerical LLM frameworks.

Forecasting & decision systems

Time-series and deep-learning forecasting, energy prediction for renewable-powered EV charging, probabilistic digital twins for microclimate, and multi-criteria decision support.
